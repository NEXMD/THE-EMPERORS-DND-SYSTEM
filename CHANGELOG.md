### Confirmed Weapon Mastery mechanics recorded
- Rapid Impact uses two separate D20 attack rolls against one target.
- The first strike receives the Quick Impact accuracy bonus; the second does not.
- Each strike's damage is resolved independently.
- The second strike proceeds after a first-strike miss.
- A defeated target cannot be replaced between strikes; the second strike is wasted if the target is already defeated.
- Players do not see exact enemy health.

### Aggro System — Version 1.0 baseline
- Established the five player-aggro tiers: Base Aggro, Player's Aggro, Player's Aggro (Stealth), Player's Aggro in Combat, Aggro Decay, and the end-of-combat reset.
- Every player begins from 100 Base Aggro.
- Player's Aggro uses the actual modified CHA score: 100 - 2 × (Modified CHA - 10). CHA-based equipment changes are applied before direct aggro item modifiers.
- Established a minimum of 30 for Player's Aggro and accumulated combat aggro; there is no maximum.
- Recorded the Stealth reduction chart from 0% to 20%, its unseen/undetected conditions, and cancellation when the player attacks, heals, or performs a support action.
- Confirmed that losing Stealth restores normal Player's Aggro before adding aggro generated by the revealing action.
- Recorded normal damage aggro at 1:1 and Tank damage aggro at 1:2.
- Established healing aggro at 1 aggro per effective HP restored; overhealing generates none, and the healer receives the aggro.
- Established HP-loss aggro per separate damage event: floor(damage received ÷ 5), rounded down for each event before adding it to accumulated combat aggro.
- Confirmed that poison, burning, and other ongoing damage-over-time effects generate no aggro.
- Established decay of 5 aggro per player turn without attacking or healing, and 5 aggro per round while unconscious.
- Confirmed that unconscious players retain aggro but cannot generate additional aggro; revival retains the remaining value.
- Confirmed that all enemies share one encounter aggro pool and that separate per-enemy aggro tables and proximity-based aggro are rejected.
- Established Low-INT-only size adjustments and the size modifier chart.
- Established the 10% enemy-instinct aggro bonus at combat start; it does not accumulate and does not force targeting. The DM selects the governing instincts in mixed-species encounters.
- Confirmed Intelligent Support Detection occurs once at the beginning of combat, uses a CHA check, and leaves the DC and failure amount to the DM.
- Approved DM taunt reference ranges: Minor +5 to +10; Moderate +11 to +20; Strong +21 to +30; Exceptional +31 to +40. Taunts do not automatically force targeting.
- Confirmed that combat aggro resets to normal Player's Aggro after the encounter.
- Preserved DM adjudication for AoE aggregation, mixed-intelligence size interactions, numerical INT thresholds, Stealth decimal rounding, and the order of combined starting adjustments.
- Removed the earlier unsupported weighted spin-wheel target-selection formula from the Aggro System documentation.
