# Changelog

All notable additions and approved mechanical changes to THE EMPEROR'S DND SYSTEM are recorded here.

## 2026-11-20

### Aggro System — full documentation recovery and correction
- Rebuilt the canonical Aggro System record around all six tiers: Base Aggro, Player's Aggro, Player's Aggro (Stealth), Player's Aggro in Combat, Aggro Decay, and End of Combat.
- Restored the weighted spin-wheel target-selection method and its proportional aggro formula. The highest-aggro player has the highest probability, not a guaranteed target.
- Corrected the aggro floors: Tier 1 Player's Aggro has a minimum of 30; Aggro Decay reduces accumulated aggro to a floor of 0.
- Recorded the approved Charisma formula using the modified actual CHA score, not the CHA modifier.
- Added the full Stealth skill-modifier reduction table and all Stealth cancellation conditions.
- Added the rule to restore normal Player's Aggro before adding aggro from the action that reveals a Stealthed player.
- Added Low-INT-only size adjustments at the beginning of combat.
- Added the non-stacking +10% enemy-instinct bonus at combat start and DM selection for mixed-species encounters.
- Added Intelligent Support Detection: one CHA check at combat start; success gives no extra aggro, failure adds a small DM-determined amount.
- Added normal-character damage aggro at 1:1 and Tank damage aggro at 1:2.
- Added effective healing at 1:1, with aggro assigned to the healer and no aggro for overhealing or the healed recipient.
- Added HP-loss aggro as floor(HP lost ÷ 5), calculated separately for each damage event.
- Confirmed that poison, burning, and other ongoing damage-over-time effects generate no aggro.
- Added all Aggro Decay action conditions, the -5 per-turn rule, the 0 floor, and the separate -5-per-round unconscious-player rule.
- Added approved DM taunt ranges: Minor +5–10; Moderate +11–20; Strong +21–30; Exceptional +31–40.
- Added enemy-instinct examples, Low-/Average-/High-INT behaviour, tie resolution, shared encounter aggro, and rejected proximity-based/per-enemy aggro tables.
- Preserved unresolved AoE, mixed-INT, revival, decay-stacking, INT-threshold, rounding, target-selection timing, and item-stacking questions rather than inventing answers.
- Added a DM Quick Reference and updated the website, root README, roadmap, and project changelog.

### Existing Weapon Mastery records retained
- Light Hammer weapon profile and its selected Rapid Impact skill remain unchanged.
- Weapon Mastery remains the current development focus.

- Preserved the original 1.0-draft structured Aggro source as an unchanged JSON archive under Aggro System/Archive.
