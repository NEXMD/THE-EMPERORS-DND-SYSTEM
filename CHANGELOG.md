# Changelog

All notable additions and approved mechanical changes to THE EMPEROR'S DND SYSTEM are recorded here.

## 2026-11-20

### Added
- Initial project handbook homepage and dark fantasy styling.
- Light Hammer weapon profile.
- Quick Impact passive trait documentation.
- Rapid Impact active skill documentation.
- Shared active-skill Action requirement.
- Initial documentation structure.

### Confirmed mechanics recorded
- Rapid Impact uses two separate D20 attack rolls against one target.
- The first strike receives the Quick Impact accuracy bonus; the second does not.
- Each strike's damage is resolved independently.
- The second strike proceeds after a first-strike miss.
- A defeated target cannot be replaced between strikes; the second strike is wasted if the target is already defeated.
- Players do not see exact enemy health.

### Not assigned
- No numerical accuracy bonus, base damage, range, or other unspecified weapon statistics were invented.
