### Confirmed mechanics recorded
- Rapid Impact uses two separate D20 attack rolls against one target.
- The first strike receives the Quick Impact accuracy bonus; the second does not.
- Each strike's damage is resolved independently.
- The second strike proceeds after a first-strike miss.
- A defeated target cannot be replaced between strikes; the second strike is wasted if the target is already defeated.
- Players do not see exact enemy health.

### Not assigned
- No numerical accuracy bonus, base damage, range, Aggro formula, Stealth reduction, or healing/support aggro values were invented.
