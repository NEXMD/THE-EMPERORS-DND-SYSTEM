# THE EMPEROR'S DND SYSTEM

A homebrew tabletop role-playing game system: its weapons, skills, combat rules, mechanics, and balance decisions.

> **Development status:** In progress. Only confirmed decisions are documented as established rules. Unspecified values remain marked as **TBD**.

## Main systems

- [Weapon Master](Weapon%20Master/README.md) — weapon catalogue, passive concepts, active skills, and mastery development. **Current development focus.**
- [Aggro System](Aggro%20System/README.md) — enemy target-priority design, recorded mechanics, and unfinished decisions. **Priority 1 on the ideas list; incomplete.**

These are separate systems. Do not assume that they interact unless the project owner approves an interaction.

## Design principles

1. Preserve decisions exactly once they are approved.
2. Do not invent numerical values where none have been established.
3. Keep proposed ideas separate from confirmed mechanics and unfinished details.
4. Do not silently change previously established mechanics.
5. Do not assume two separate systems interact unless the project owner approves it.
6. Record additions and approved changes in the changelog.
