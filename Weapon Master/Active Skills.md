# Active Weapon Skills

This record contains the active skills documented in the Master Handover Document, plus the Light Hammer decision made later in the current project conversation.

**Universal activation rule:** Every active weapon skill consumes an **Action**.

Numerical damage, durations, thresholds, and other details remain unfinished wherever they have not been decided.

## 1. Light Crossbow — Crippling Shot

**Description:** The wielder steadies their crossbow, concentrates and fires a precision shot at an enemy's vulnerable body parts.

**Activation:** Action.

**Conditions:**
- The wielder must not move from their current position.
- The wielder must be able to concentrate while aiming.

**Effect:** Roll 1d4.

| Roll | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 4 | Headshot — deals bonus damage. |
| 3 | Arm Shot — outcome depends on the target's equipment. |
| 2 | Leg Shot — reduces the target's movement speed by 75%. |
| 1 | Miss — the shot misses the intended target. |

### Arm Shot details
- If the enemy wears armor, the shot hits their arm and forces them to drop their weapon. Damage is reduced.
- If the enemy holds a shield, the arrow bypasses the shield and hits the enemy directly.
- The armored arm shot is intended to cause enough pain or damage to justify dropping the weapon.

**Unfinished:** Exact damage, minimum damage threshold, and other numerical details.

## 2. Dart — Distracting Throw

**Description:** The wielder throws a dart to distract an enemy and create an opening.

**Activation:** Action.

**Condition:** The wielder must not be seen by the enemy.

**Effect:**
- The targeted enemy becomes distracted.
- The wielder and their allies gain Advantage on their next attack against that enemy.

This is a team-support ability, not merely a personal Advantage ability.

## 3. Shortbow — Rain of Arrows

**Description:** The wielder fires a volley of arrows at every enemy they choose to target.

**Activation:** Action.

**Mechanics:**
- The player chooses which creatures they consider enemies.
- Each selected enemy receives 3 arrows.
- Every arrow has Advantage on its attack roll.
- Roll twice for each arrow and use the higher result.
- Each arrow is resolved independently.
- Range is based on the Shortbow's normal weapon range.

**Example:** If the player selects 3 enemies, they fire 9 arrows in total, with Advantage on every attack roll.

The player chooses the enemies, and every selected enemy receives 3 arrows.

## 6. Dagger — Prep Time

**Description:** The wielder prepares their dagger by coating it with all available poisons in their inventory.

**Activation:** Action.

**Condition:** The player must possess poison.

**Mechanics:**
- Applies all available poisons to the dagger.
- Multiple different poison types can be stacked on the same dagger.
- Each successful dagger strike inflicts the corresponding poison effects on the enemy.
- Each inflicted damage-over-time (DoT) effect lasts 5 rounds.
- The poison coating itself expires after 4 rounds.

**Important distinction:**
- The coating lasts 4 rounds.
- Poison effects already inflicted on enemies continue for their full 5-round duration, even after the coating expires.

### Planned shop items

The user intends to introduce multiple poison items through a shop.

The item naming concept is **Vial of [Poison Type]**.

Examples suggested but not finalized:
- Vial of Venom.
- Vial of Paralysis.
- Vial of Weakening.
- Vial of Slumber.

The poison catalogue and individual poison mechanics will be developed separately.

## 8. Handaxe — Finishing Hatchet

**Description:** The wielder attempts to execute a severely weakened enemy with a brutal hatchet strike.

**Activation:** Action.

**Target:** One enemy.

**Condition:** Target must be below 10% HP.

**Effects:**
- Successful hit: Deals bonus damage.
- Miss: The wielder becomes stunned for 1 round.

The 10% threshold was selected because the ability should not be too powerful.

Exact bonus damage and stun details are unfinished.

## 9. Javelin — Piercing Launch

**Description:** The wielder launches their javelin with tremendous force, attempting to penetrate defensive barriers and pierce through multiple enemies.

**Activation:** Action.

**Targeting:** Straight line.

**Mechanics:**
- The javelin can potentially pierce through multiple enemies.
- Whether it penetrates a shield depends on the attack's D20 roll.
- A sufficiently high roll can penetrate an ordinary shield.
- Magical shields require a higher roll.
- If the attack does not roll high enough to penetrate a shield, the shield absorbs part of the damage.
- If the javelin successfully penetrates the shield, it can potentially continue through the target and hit additional enemies behind them.

Shield penetration depends on the D20 result rather than being automatic.

**Unfinished:**
- Exact penetration thresholds.
- Damage absorbed by shields.
- Whether damage changes against subsequent enemies.
- How different magical defenses are classified.

## 10. Light Hammer — Rapid Impact

**Status:** Selected in the current project conversation.

**Description:** Deliver two consecutive hammer strikes against one enemy.

**Activation:** Action.

**Target:** One enemy.

**Mechanics:**
- Make two separate D20 attack rolls, one for each strike.
- Resolve each strike's damage independently.
- The first strike receives the Quick Impact accuracy bonus.
- The second strike does not receive the Quick Impact accuracy bonus.
- If the first strike misses, the second strike still proceeds.
- If the first strike defeats the target, the second strike is wasted.
- The second strike cannot be redirected to another enemy.
- Players do not see an enemy's exact health value.

### Possible roll outcomes

| First strike | Second strike | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Hit | Hit | Both strikes connect. |
| Hit | Miss | Only the first connects. |
| Miss | Hit | Only the second connects. |
| Miss | Miss | Neither connects. |

### Passive — Quick Impact

Gain an accuracy bonus on the first attack against an enemy each turn.

### Unspecified values

- Exact accuracy bonus.
- Base damage.
- Range.
- Other weapon statistics.

Do not assign these values until they are decided.
