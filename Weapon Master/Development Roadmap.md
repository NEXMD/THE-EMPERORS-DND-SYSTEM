## Current focus

Active weapon skill design is in progress. Nine skills were documented in the handover record. Light Hammer's active skill has since been selected as **Rapid Impact** in the current project conversation.

## Balancing rule

Do not balance individual abilities prematurely. Complete the weapon abilities first, then review the entire system before balancing. The selected base damage approach is the Hybrid System (Option C): standard D&D 5e (2014) weapon damage dice are the foundation, with possible weapon-specific modifications where identity calls for them.

## Design principles

- Every weapon should have a reason to be used beyond its damage dice.
- Abilities should create meaningful tactical choices.
- Some abilities can have drawbacks or conditions.
- Mechanics may interact with shields, armor, barriers, positioning, and enemy states where approved.
- Keep the system interesting without making combat unnecessarily difficult to track.
- Do not establish a mechanic without the user's approval.
