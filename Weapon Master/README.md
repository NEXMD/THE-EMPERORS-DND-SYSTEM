# Weapon Mastery & Weapon-Specific Skills

**System status:** In development  
**Current development stage:** Designing active weapon skills  
**Balancing:** Deliberately postponed

## Core concept

Every weapon has:
- Its own passive ability.
- Its own active skill.
- A distinct combat identity.

The purpose is to encourage players to think strategically about which weapon they use. Weapons should not be interchangeable damage tools.

## Universal rules

### Active skills
Every active weapon skill consumes an **Action**. This is a universal rule and does not need to be re-decided for each weapon.

### Base weapon damage — Hybrid System (Option C)
- Standard D&D 5e (2014) weapon damage dice are the foundation.
- Individual weapons may receive modifications where their identity calls for it.
- Damage values are not being assigned or balanced yet.

### Balancing
Balancing is postponed until all weapons and their abilities have been developed. The complete system should be reviewed before individual abilities are balanced.

The weapon catalogue and passive concepts have been selected. Passive mechanics still need refinement. Active weapon skill design is in progress.

Nine active skills were documented in the handover record. Light Hammer's active skill has since been selected as **Rapid Impact** in the current project conversation and is documented separately.

## Design boundaries

- Do not invent or assign unapproved mechanics.
- Keep confirmed rules separate from suggestions and unfinished details.
- Do not assume Weapon Mastery interacts with the Aggro System unless that interaction is approved.
- Do not begin overall balancing before the designated balancing stage.
