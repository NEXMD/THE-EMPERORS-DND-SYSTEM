# Weapon Catalogue

The catalogue uses the classic D&D 5e (2014) weapon list: **37 weapons**.

The passive names and descriptions below are the selected concepts. Some exact mechanics still need development. An unnamed concept remains unnamed until the project owner decides otherwise.

## Simple Ranged

| Weapon | Passive | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| Light Crossbow | Steady Aim | If the wielder does not move during their turn, their next attack gains an accuracy bonus. |
| Dart | Rapid Throw | After hitting with a dart, the next dart attack against the same enemy gains an accuracy bonus. |
| Shortbow | Unnamed | Gain an accuracy bonus when attacking an enemy the wielder has not attacked before. |
| Sling | Concussive Impact | Critical hits can briefly disorient the target. |

## Simple Melee

| Weapon | Passive | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| Club | Blunt Force | Attacks have a chance to briefly reduce the target's movement speed. |
| Dagger | Backstabber | Gain an accuracy bonus when attacking an enemy unaware of the wielder's presence. |
| Greatclub | Overwhelming Force | Gain a damage bonus against targets smaller than the wielder. |
| Handaxe | Cleave | After killing an enemy, the next melee attack gains an attack bonus. |
| Javelin | Piercing Momentum | Gain a damage bonus when throwing from at least 20 feet away. |
| Light Hammer | Quick Impact | Gain an accuracy bonus on the first attack against an enemy each turn. |
| Mace | Unyielding Strike | Attacks cannot have their damage reduced by ordinary physical armor. |
| Quarterstaff | Counterbalance | Gain an accuracy bonus on attacks made immediately after successfully avoiding an attack. |
| Sickle | Blood Scent | Gain a damage bonus against enemies already suffering from bleeding. |
| Spear | Keep Your Distance | Gain an accuracy bonus against enemies that moved at least 10 feet toward the wielder during the turn. |
