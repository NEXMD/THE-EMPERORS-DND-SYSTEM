WEAPON MASTERY

ONE WEAPON. ONE IDENTITY. A DIFFERENT WAY TO FIGHT.

Every weapon has its own passive ability and active skill. The goal is to make weapon choice a meaningful tactical decision rather than a simple choice of damage dice.

SYSTEM FOUNDATION

Core Rules

The currently recorded foundations of Weapon Mastery.

RULE 01

Unique weapon identity

Every weapon has its own passive ability, active skill, and combat identity.

CORE CONCEPT
RULE 02

Active skills cost an Action

Every active weapon skill consumes an Action.

UNIVERSAL RULE
RULE 03

Hybrid damage foundation

Standard D&D 5e (2014) damage dice are the foundation. Weapon-specific changes may be considered later.
