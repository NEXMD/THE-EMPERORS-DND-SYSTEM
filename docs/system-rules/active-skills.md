# Active Skills

## Universal action requirement

Every active weapon skill consumes an **Action**.

This is an established system-wide rule. Individual weapon records should specify their active skill's target, resolution, and any additional restrictions.

## Documentation standard

For each active skill, record:

- Action cost
- Target selection and target limits
- Attack rolls or other resolution method
- Damage or effect resolution
- What happens on a miss or failed check
- Restrictions between sequential effects

Do not assume numerical bonuses, damage values, ranges, or cooldowns unless they have been explicitly established.
