AGGRO SYSTEM

WHO BECOMES THE ENEMY'S PRIORITY?

Aggro determines each player's probability of being selected as an enemy target. Damage, healing, Charisma, creature size and Stealth all contribute to the system.

CORE RULES ESTABLISHED Some implementation details remain open. Weapon Mastery is the current development focus.

01 / AGGRO CALCULATION

The Formula

The Charisma formula and cumulative damage/healing rules were established in the earlier discussion.

APPROVED AGGRO CALCULATION

A = 100 - 2 × (CHA - 10) + S + D + H

VariableMeaning
100Every player starts with 100 base aggro.
CHAActual Charisma score, not the Charisma modifier.
SApplicable creature-size modifier, only against enemies with low Intelligence.
DTotal damage dealt by that player during the fight.
HTotal HP restored by that player through healing during the fight.

CHA 10 is neutral. Each point above 10 reduces starting aggro by 2; each point below 10 increases it by 2.

02 / COMBAT ACCUMULATION

Damage & Healing

Both values accumulate throughout the entire fight, not just during the current turn.

Combat eventAggro gainedWho receives it?
Deal 1 damage+1The attacker
Restore 1 HP through healing+1The healer
Receive healing0The recipient receives no aggro from that healing

Healing aggro belongs entirely to the healer. The healed player does not receive the aggro generated by the restored HP.

03 / CHARACTER ATTRIBUTE

Charisma

  • Use the actual Charisma ability score, not its modifier.
  • CHA 10 is the neutral reference point.
  • Every point above CHA 10 reduces starting aggro by 2.
  • Every point below CHA 10 increases starting aggro by 2.

The Charisma formula is an established rule, not a pending proposal.

04 / CREATURE SIZE

Size Modifier

This modifier applies only when the enemy making the target decision has low Intelligence.

Against enemies that do not have low Intelligence, the size modifier does not apply.

05 / STEALTH

Remaining Unseen

  • Stealth reduces aggro while the player remains unseen.
  • The Stealth reduction stacks with the Charisma effect.
  • Physical contact cancels the Stealth-based aggro reduction.
  • If an enemy notices the player, the Stealth-based reduction is cancelled.

The exact numerical Stealth reduction is still to be specified.

06 / TARGET SELECTION

Weighted Spin Wheel

Enemy targeting is weighted by each party member's current aggro.

PLAYER SELECTION PROBABILITY

P = (Player Aggro ÷ Total Party Aggro) × 100%

  1. Determine each party member's current aggro.
  2. Add all party members' aggro values to get total party aggro.
  3. Assign each player a wheel share proportional to their aggro divided by total party aggro.
  4. Use the weighted spin wheel to select the enemy's target.

The player with the highest aggro has the greatest chance of being selected, but is not guaranteed to be selected. A healer can become the most likely target if their accumulated healing aggro exceeds another player's accumulated damage aggro.

An aggro change adjusts the player's probability; it does not automatically force the enemy to switch targets.

07 / REMAINING DETAILS

Still to Be Specified

  • The exact numerical amount by which Stealth reduces aggro.
  • Exactly when or how often the weighted spin wheel is used to select or reselect a target.
  • Whether non-healing support actions generate aggro, and if so, how much.
  • Any additional modifiers or special-case interactions.
  • How accumulated values are handled between separate fights.

Damage, healing, Charisma, creature-size modifiers and weighted target selection are established mechanics. They are not pending decisions.
