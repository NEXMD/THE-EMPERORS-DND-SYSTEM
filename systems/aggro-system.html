SYSTEM 02 · PRIORITY 01

AGGRO SYSTEM

WHO BECOMES THE ENEMY'S PRIORITY?

Every player has an aggro value that influences how likely enemies are to target them. The goal is to make enemy targeting more dynamic than simply attacking whoever is closest.

DESIGN RECORD · INCOMPLETE Weapon Mastery remains the current development focus.

01 / FOUNDATION

Base Aggro

100BASE AGGRO PER PLAYER

Every player starts with 100 base aggro. Other recorded factors can modify a player's aggro value.

03 / CHARACTER ATTRIBUTE

Charisma

  • Use the actual Charisma ability score, not the Charisma modifier.
  • Higher Charisma lowers aggro.
  • A Charisma score of 10 is the neutral reference point.
PROPOSED FORMULA · NOT CONFIRMED

Aggro = 100 - 2 × (CHA - 10)

The formula above was discussed as a proposal. It is not a finalized rule and must not be treated as confirmed.

04 / STEALTH

Remaining Unseen

  • Stealth can lower aggro.
  • The effect applies only while the player remains unseen.
  • Physical contact cancels the stealth-based aggro reduction.
  • Stealth and Charisma can stack.
  • Stealth is automatically cancelled if an enemy notices the player.

The exact numerical Stealth reduction has not been decided.

05 / SUPPORT ACTIONS

Healing & Support

Healing and support actions can attract enemy attention. A healer who heals or supports allies can become a target. The design intent is for healers to face the risk of attracting aggro.

The exact aggro changes from healing and support have not been decided.

06 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Keep Targeting Manageable

  • The system should be manageable for both players and the DM.
  • Avoid mechanics that require excessive tracking.
  • Small changes in aggro should not automatically change the enemy's target.

07 / OPEN DECISIONS

Still to Be Defined

  • Final Charisma formula.
  • Exact numerical Stealth reduction.
  • Exact aggro changes from healing and support.
  • Complete target-selection procedure.
  • Other aggro modifiers and interactions.

This is an incomplete design record. The Aggro System and Weapon Mastery remain separate; no interaction between them is assumed.
