WEAPON RECORD 001

LIGHT HAMMER

QUICK IMPACT · RAPID IMPACT

WEAPON IDENTITY

Fast, precise, consecutive strikes

The Light Hammer's established identity centres on landing an accurate opening attack and following through with a rapid second strike.

PASSIVE TRAIT

Quick Impact

Gain an accuracy bonus on the first attack against an enemy each turn.

ACTIVE SKILL · RAPID IMPACT

Two strikes. One target.

Deliver two consecutive hammer strikes against one enemy. Each strike is resolved separately.

CONFIRMED SKILL RULES

Rapid Impact — Resolution

  1. Using Rapid Impact consumes an Action.
  2. Choose one enemy as the target.
  3. Make two separate D20 attack rolls, one for each strike.
  4. Resolve each strike's damage independently.
  5. The first strike receives the Quick Impact accuracy bonus. The second strike does not.
  6. If the first strike misses, the second strike still proceeds.
  7. If the first strike defeats the target, the second strike is wasted. It cannot be redirected to another enemy.

POSSIBLE ROLL OUTCOMES

Two independent attacks

  • Hit / Hit — both strikes connect.
  • Hit / Miss — the first connects; the second misses.
  • Miss / Hit — the first misses; the second connects.
  • Miss / Miss — neither strike connects.

The two rolls are independent. A miss on the first strike does not cancel the second strike.

PLAYER INFORMATION

Enemy health is hidden

Players do not see an enemy's exact health value. They therefore cannot reliably know whether the first strike was lethal before the second strike resolves. If the target has already been defeated, the second strike is still wasted and cannot switch targets.
